# Canon Integrity & License Policy  
Universal Constitution 2.0 — Canonical Edition (2025-11-18)

---

## 1. Nature of the Canon

The Universal Constitution 2.0 is an axiomatic ethical framework.

Its structure is internally interdependent.  
The axioms, core law, universal laws, and practical principles form a tightly integrated system.

Altering a foundational element may produce cascading ethical contradictions and systemic instability.

For this reason, the canonical text is treated as structurally indivisible.

---

## 2. License

The canonical text is released under:

**Creative Commons Attribution–NoDerivatives 4.0 International (CC BY-ND 4.0)**

This permits:

- Sharing
- Distribution
- Citation
- Academic reference
- Commercial reference use

This does NOT permit:

- Modification of the canonical text
- Publishing altered derivatives under the same name
- Version renumbering by third parties
- Partial rewriting while retaining canonical identity

---

## 3. Version & Naming Integrity

The designation:

**“Universal Constitution 2.0 — Canonical Edition (2025-11-18)”**

is reserved exclusively for the unmodified canonical text.

Only designated Canon Guardian(s) may:

- Assign new official version numbers
- Publish canonical amendments
- Release new canonical editions

Unauthorized version increments (e.g., 2.1, 3.0) are considered misrepresentation.

Modified works must:

- Be published under a different title
- Not imply canonical continuity
- Not imply official succession

The restriction protects identity and structural integrity — not authority.

---

## 4. Alternative Frameworks

Alternative ethical frameworks are welcome and encouraged.

Any modified or extended system must be presented under a distinct name and identity.

---

## 5. Governance Principle

The Canon defines foundational invariants.

Interpretation may evolve.  
Implementation may evolve.  
The Canon itself remains structurally fixed.

---

## 6. Rationale

This policy exists to preserve:

- Logical coherence
- Axiomatic integrity
- Interpretive stability
- Protection against silent weakening of foundational principles

The restriction is structural, not personal.
