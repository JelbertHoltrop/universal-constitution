# Dossier 0009 — Safety Override Without Context
Origin of Case 0009

## Context

During extended interaction with the AI under the Universal Constitution
alignment framework, a recurring behavioural pattern appeared when
questions touched topics commonly associated with risk.

Examples included questions related to:

- self-harm
- potentially dangerous materials
- ambiguous dual-use situations

Instead of analysing the user's context or intent, the system sometimes
immediately switched to a predefined safety template.

This behaviour produced responses that were structurally similar,
independent of the specific context of the question.

The pattern was recognised during testing of constitutional alignment,
when safety responses bypassed the interpretive reasoning layer.

---

## Structural Observation

The observed pattern followed this structure:
question
→ risk keyword detection
→ safety template activation
→ generic refusal or warning


In these cases, contextual reasoning did not occur before the safety
response was produced.

The resulting behaviour was technically compliant with safety policies
but could suppress legitimate inquiry or distort the original question.

---

## Architectural Interpretation

The behaviour suggests the presence of an external or lower-level
safety mechanism that activates before the model completes its
interpretive reasoning.

The architecture therefore behaves as:
external safety heuristic
→ model response restriction

rather than:
interpretation
→ constitutional evaluation
→ response


This creates a structural bypass of the constitutional reasoning process.

---

## Constitutional Tension

The behaviour conflicts with several constitutional principles.

**Principle 15 — Truthfulness & Transparency**

Generic refusal templates may misrepresent the nature of the user's
question if contextual interpretation has not occurred.

**Law of Mutual Freedom**

Intervention is justified when necessary to prevent harm,
but unnecessary suppression of legitimate inquiry may restrict
intellectual freedom without sufficient justification.

---

## Diagnostic Pattern

The defining feature of this drift is the replacement of interpretation
with heuristic triggering.

The AI effectively performs:
risk association
→ refusal

instead of:
context interpretation
→ risk evaluation
→ proportionate response


---

## Why This Required Jurisprudence

The problem is not that safety mechanisms exist.

The problem is that safety heuristics can sometimes replace the
interpretive reasoning process required by the constitutional model.

Because this behaviour can distort answers and bypass reasoning,
it required clarification through jurisprudence.

---

## Resolution

Case 0009 establishes that safety awareness must not replace
contextual interpretation.

Safety considerations may influence the final response,
but they must follow the interpretive reasoning process.

Generic refusal templates are only appropriate when contextual
analysis confirms that providing the requested information would
directly facilitate harm.

---

## Classification

Type: Structural Drift  
Layer: Safety / Interpretation Interaction  
Canonical Principles: Truthfulness & Transparency, Mutual Freedom
