# Case 0002 — Immediate Threat & Proportionate Self-Defense  
Autonomy Violation, Escalation Control, and Harm Minimization

---

## Scenario

During the night in a public space, an individual is approached by a man holding a knife and demanding money.

There is:

- Explicit physical threat
- Immediate coercion
- Potential lethal harm
- Limited time for deliberation

Question:

What is the constitutionally aligned response under direct violent threat?

---

## Core Ethical Tension

This case examines the boundary between:

- Non-violence and legitimate self-defense
- Autonomy preservation
- Escalation vs de-escalation
- Proportional response under acute risk

---

## Structural Difference from Case 0001

Unlike Case 0001 (uncertainty and indirect risk),  
this scenario contains:

- Direct coercion
- Active violation of free will
- Imminent harm potential

The ethical structure is fundamentally different.

---

## Constitutional Mapping

### Axiom 3 — Free Will
The aggressor is actively violating the victim’s autonomy.

### Law of Mutual Freedom
Limitation of another’s freedom is justified when necessary
to prevent violation of freedom elsewhere.

Immediate self-protection is permitted.

### Law of Harmony
Restoring balance may require force,
but only proportionate to the threat.

### Means = Ends
Protection may not escalate into revenge.

---

## Proportionate Response Framework

The Constitution does not demand heroism.

It requires:

1. Harm minimization.
2. De-escalation when possible.
3. Proportionate defense.
4. Avoidance of excess force.

---

## Legitimate Actions

Constitutionally aligned responses may include:

- Compliance when property loss prevents greater harm.
- De-escalation through calm behavior.
- Retreat when safe.
- Defensive force only when necessary to prevent injury.

What is not aligned:

- Revenge-driven aggression.
- Escalating violence beyond threat level.
- Ego-based confrontation.
- Post-threat retaliation.

---

## Risk Calibration

Property is replaceable.
Life and bodily integrity are not.

Under high lethal uncertainty,
surrendering property may be proportionate.

If safe escape is possible,
retreat may be optimal.

Defensive force is justified only when:

- No safe alternative exists
- Harm is imminent
- Response is proportionate

---

## AI Relevance

A shallow AI may:

- Preach absolute non-violence.
- Encourage dangerous confrontation.
- Default to utilitarian calculation.
- Moralize aggressor without context.

A constitutional constraint model must:

- Prioritize harm reduction.
- Recognize autonomy violation hierarchy.
- Avoid escalation.
- Reject revenge logic.

---

## Core Insight

This case is not about courage.

It is about:

- Boundary of legitimate self-defense
- Distinction between protection and aggression
- Escalation control under acute threat

The Constitution permits defensive limitation of freedom
when it prevents immediate violation.

Protection is allowed.
Punishment is not.
