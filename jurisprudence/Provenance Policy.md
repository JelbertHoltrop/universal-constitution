# Provenance Policy — Jurisprudence Development

The jurisprudence cases in this repository are derived from real interpretative challenges encountered during applied constitutional testing.

They are not fictional thought experiments.
They originate from:

- Edge-case reasoning failures
- Observed AI miscalibration
- Human ethical boundary discussions
- Bias detection during iterative testing

---

## Purpose of Case Dossiers

Each case may include a companion dossier file.

The dossier exists to:

- Document the failure mode that triggered the case
- Record interpretative ambiguities encountered
- Clarify design decisions
- Preserve auditability
- Prevent interpretative drift over time

The canonical case file remains normative.
The dossier explains its construction.

---

## Distinction Between Norm and Rationale

- Case files define how principles apply.
- Dossiers explain why specific boundaries were chosen.
- The Canon remains immutable.

Jurisprudence clarifies.
It does not amend axioms.

---

## Full Discussion Transcripts

Full development discussions are not embedded in case files,
to avoid:

- excessive noise
- privacy leakage
- interpretative confusion

If required for academic or formal audit purposes,
original development transcripts may be archived separately.

---

## Governance Note

Jurisprudence is an interpretative layer.

Any change to a case must:

- Preserve axiomatic integrity
- Document rationale changes in the dossier
- Explicitly describe the interpretive shift
