# Jurisprudence Method

The jurisprudence folder does not create new laws.

It clarifies application boundaries of the Canon under complex conditions.

Each case must:

1. Identify the structural ethical tension.
2. Distinguish statistical information from moral certainty.
3. Prevent causal inflation.
4. Preserve agency hierarchy.
5. Apply proportionality.

Jurisprudence may deepen interpretation,
but may not modify axioms.

If a case appears to contradict an axiom,
the interpretation must be reconsidered.

Axioms remain invariant.
